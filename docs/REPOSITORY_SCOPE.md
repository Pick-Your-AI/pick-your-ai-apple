# Repository scope and decision status

The maintainer requested creation of separate Apple and Android repositories according to their delivery Projects. `Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple` is the Apple/iOS client repository created under that instruction. [Project 5](https://github.com/orgs/Pick-Your-AI/projects/5) and [Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple#1](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple/issues/1) remain the delivery authorities.

The [Apple platform/framework/repository ADR](decisions/issue-22-platform.md) is accepted for engineering implementation. [Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple#22](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple/issues/22) is closed with an [exact-artifact independent delegated receiving receipt](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple/issues/22#issuecomment-6244062120) for its three decision-artifact criteria. The selected baseline is SwiftUI with UIKit interoperability, Swift 6, iPhone and adaptive iPad, iOS/iPadOS 17.0 minimum, and system SQLite for local drafts and revision history.

Follow the ADR's App/Core/Data module boundaries and the [checked-in toolchain pin](../App/toolchain.json) for dependent implementation. The [foundation disposition and subsequent receiving receipts](decisions/foundation-disposition.md#subsequent-receiving-receipts) distinguish this accepted engineering decision from downstream app/runtime, physical-device, service, signing and release acceptance. Production application identity, Developer Team and App Store Connect configuration, service hosting, operations responsibility and delivery commitments retain their separate decisions and evidence requirements. Apple work does not automatically include additional Apple platforms. Preserve existing issue deliverable paths.

The existing website remains static. Shared-service placement requires [Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-android#35](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-android/issues/35) and Apple integration requires [Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple#23](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple/issues/23).
